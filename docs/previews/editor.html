Entry

Walked to the market before the city woke up. The air had that first-autumn edge to it, and the stalls were still half asleep under their lamps.

I bought too many persimmons and a bunch of eucalyptus for the kitchen table. No plan for either — just the pleasure of choosing something because it looked good in the morning light.

On the way home I took the long way past the park.
